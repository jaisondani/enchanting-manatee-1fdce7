How It Began

In 2010, two technologists with a shared obsession for clean architecture began building data systems in a small Chennai office. What started as a passion project evolved into Danielle Insights, formally incorporated in 2026. Today we serve enterprises across India, the Middle East, and beyond—delivering analytics, integrations, and audits that move the needle.
