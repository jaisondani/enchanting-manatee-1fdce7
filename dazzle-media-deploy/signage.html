Frequently Asked Questions – Signage

1. I don't have a design. Can you help?

▼

Absolutely! Our in‑house design team in Bangalore works closely with you to turn your ideas into a professional signage concept.

2. How much does a custom sign cost?

▼

It depends on the type, size, materials, and complexity. We provide a detailed, no‑obligation quote tailored to your budget and location.

3. What's the typical timeline?

▼

For non‑illuminated signs: 2‑3 days. For illuminated/complex projects: 5‑7 days. We'll give you a clear schedule upfront.

4. ACP vs acrylic letters – what's the difference?

▼

ACP boards are sleek, flat panels for large facades. Acrylic letters are raised, giving a premium 3D look for storefronts and reception walls.

5. Are your outdoor signs weather‑proof?

▼

Yes! We use marine‑grade aluminium and UV‑resistant paints that handle rain, sun, and dust. Our signs in Sudhama Nagar and Commercial Street have been standing strong for years.

6. Can I see samples of your work?

▼

Of course. We have a portfolio of recent projects across Bengaluru. You can also visit our Office and workshop to see materials and finishes in person.

7. Do you handle BBMP permits?

▼

Yes, we handle the entire permit process. Please inform us before starting if you require this service.

8. Do you offer after‑sales support?

▼

Absolutely. We professionally install every sign and stand behind our work. We're just a call away – even years after installation.

9. Can you make large‑format hoardings?

▼

Yes, we have the in‑house capacity to produce massive hoardings and pylon signs for builders and developers in Bengaluru's IT parks and residential projects.

10. Can you rush an order?

▼

We can often expedite simple projects. Just let us know your deadline, and we'll do our best to meet it without compromising on quality.
